Questions

Is this the same app as Resume Builder?

No — it's a separate app and a separate one-time purchase, built on the same engine. They share template designs so your resume and cover letter match, and each site links to the other.

Do I need to be a good writer?

No. You answer short prompts about the job and your experience; the builder turns them into a structured letter. You can edit every sentence before exporting.

What does the free version include?

Five templates, all three tones, unlimited letters, and A4 PDF export — free forever, no account and no ads. Premium unlocks the full template library for a single $9 payment.

Where are my letters stored?

On your device only. The builder works fully offline and nothing you write is uploaded anywhere.

Can I reuse a letter for another job?

Yes — duplicate any saved letter, swap the company and role, adjust the middle paragraph, and export a fresh copy in under a minute.
